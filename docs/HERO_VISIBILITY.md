# Desktop hero and mobile download layout

The desktop hero remains a two-column composition in every downloader state:
large heading and link capsule on the left, decorative phone and information card
on the right. The primary analyze button sits inside the desktop link capsule.
The active result panel appears below the hero without a duplicate phone mockup.

Below 1024px both phone mockups are hidden in every state. The heading, URL field,
analysis/cancellation controls and result panel remain available. The result
card retains its backend-provided source thumbnail. Mobile primary buttons fill
the available width.

The supplied screenshot guides composition only. Unsupported format, privacy,
speed, watermark and quality claims are not copied. The existing local lifestyle
poster remains decorative marketing artwork, not the analyzed video.

Browser coverage verifies zero visible phones on mobile while downloading, one
visible hero phone on desktop through ready/download/completed states, source
thumbnail handling, responsive bounds, focus and download behavior. Captures are
available in `docs/screenshots/ready-{390,768,1024,1440}.png` and
`docs/screenshots/state-{idle,ready,downloading,completed}.png`.

# TikSaveMp4: 90-day off-site SEO plan

Audience: US technology readers and creators who need to save content they have permission to use. Work starts after DNS/TLS deployment and an owner-reviewed legal/contact setup. No accounts, submissions, outreach or backlinks were created by this task.

Accurate directory description: **TikSaveMp4 is an independent web tool for converting supported public TikTok videos to available MP4 video or MP3 audio. Paste a link, review the offered options and download. Formats and quality depend on the source. Private and restricted posts are unsupported, and users need permission to save and reuse content.** Website: `https://tiksavemp4.online/`.

## Days 1–30: foundation

- Week 1: verify Google Search Console and Bing, submit the sitemap, record an initial search/traffic baseline, and resolve crawl errors. Publish a real monitored contact channel and confirm the existing responsible-use/legal drafts reflect actual operations.
- Week 2: manually create consistent brand profiles on relevant platforms the owner will maintain, using the same name, URL, description and independent-service status. Do not invent a US business location, address, phone number or customer reviews.
- Weeks 3–4: identify legitimate web-tool directories and product discovery platforms with editorial standards and relevant visitors. Confirm each accepts this tool type and follow submission rules. Submit a small, carefully reviewed set; do not buy guaranteed links or promise approval.
- Keep a private outreach ledger: destination/domain, relevance, contact source, date, consent/status, response and eventual aggregate referral results. Do not publish recipients' contact information.

## Days 31–60: useful content and outreach

- Create original guides that answer actual user questions: MP4 versus MP3, why source qualities vary, where browsers save files, why public links fail, and permission/attribution basics. Show only tested behavior; legal content stays general and links to authoritative guidance.
- Have the owner review guides and examples before publication. These future guides are not included in the current sitemap until implemented as substantive public pages.
- Research relevant technology editors, creator-resource sites and resource-page maintainers. Read their editorial/submission requirements before a tailored message.
- Offer independent review with no paid-link or favorable-rating requirement. Suggest a guide/tool only when it serves the audience. Share guides in communities that permit them, disclose ownership and answer questions without dropping repetitive links.
- Send a modest batch manually, record outcomes and stop on refusal. At most one considerate follow-up if permitted; respect no-contact requests.

## Days 61–90: authority and learning

- Publish original, reproducible technical insights using owned or permitted samples: source-dependent formats, browser handling or temporary-file lifecycle. Describe methodology and limitations. Do not turn private user URLs, metadata or jobs into a public dataset.
- Build a useful, linkable format/quality/responsible-downloading guide; update it when tested behavior changes.
- Find broken or outdated resource links manually. Offer TikSaveMp4 only when it genuinely replaces the missing resource; identify the exact broken page honestly.
- Continue relevant outreach and update useful content based on feedback. Review referral sessions, actual successful analyses/deliveries, US search queries and indexing against the baseline. Favor useful audiences over raw backlink totals.
- At day 90, document what actually happened, including refusals and unknowns. Continue channels that bring real users; no guaranteed rankings, backlink quota or traffic target is promised.

## Link standards

Prefer natural anchors: TikSaveMp4, TikSaveMp4 online downloader, tiksavemp4.online, or this TikTok download tool. Exact-match wording should be rare and editorially natural. Recipients choose their own wording and whether to link. Honor sponsored/nofollow treatment when applicable.

Never purchase bulk backlinks, use automated backlink software or private blog networks, spam comments/Reddit/Quora/forums, exchange links at scale, create fake reviews/listings, submit to unrelated directories, demand misleading anchor text, build thousands of links quickly or promise Google rankings. Evaluate directory audience and editorial quality before spending time or money.
